axge(4): keep four transmit transfers in flight

The transmit path allocates a single bulk OUT transfer and gates its write
callback on IFF_DRV_OACTIVE.  The flag is set as soon as a batch of packets is
submitted and cleared only when that transfer completes, so for the whole time
a batch is on the wire the driver declines further work and nothing is
assembled behind it.  The endpoint falls idle between batches.

This is the same shape as the receive limitation addressed earlier in this bug,
where a single bulk IN transfer left the endpoint idle between completions.

Allocate AXGE_N_TX_XFER transmit transfers and start, stall and stop all of
them.  The OACTIVE gate goes with it.  With one transfer that flag was the
serialisation, but with several it stops the remaining transfers picking up
work while the first is in flight, which defeats the point of having them.  The
send queue emptying ends the assembly loop on its own, so nothing is required
in its place.  if_ure(4) keeps URE_MAX_TX transfers and is arranged the same
way.

Measured on an AX88179B at gigabit, transmit went from 250 to 465 Mbit/s.  That
figure was taken over a WAN link through NAT and a VLAN tag rather than in
isolation, so it indicates the direction rather than the ceiling.
